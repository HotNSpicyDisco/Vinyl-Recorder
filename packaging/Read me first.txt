VINYL RECORDER
Record your records, look them up on Discogs, split them into tracks and save tagged FLAC/MP3 files.
https://github.com/HotNSpicyDisco/Vinyl-Recorder


GETTING STARTED - WINDOWS

1. Unzip this folder somewhere it can stay, e.g. Documents\Vinyl Recorder.
2. Double-click "Vinyl Recorder.exe".
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (Only the first time. The app just isn't signed with a paid certificate.)
3. It offers to add itself to the Start menu and desktop. Say Yes.


GETTING STARTED - MAC

1. Drag "Vinyl Recorder" into your Applications folder.
2. Open it. macOS will say it can't be opened because the developer can't be verified.
   Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway".
   (Older macOS: right-click the app > Open > Open.)
3. When it asks to use the microphone, click OK - that's how it records your audio interface.


CONNECT DISCOGS (free)

- On discogs.com go to Settings > Developers > "Generate new token".
- In Vinyl Recorder: File > Settings, paste the token, OK.
  Your collection then appears on the Album tab.


SET UP YOUR AUDIO (Record tab)

- Device: pick your audio interface. On Windows the "ASIO" entry is best if your
  interface has one (install the maker's driver first).
- Left / Right input: the input numbers your turntable or mixer is plugged into.
- Press Apply, play a record, and set your mixer so peaks reach about -6 dB
  and the clip counter stays at 0.
- If the device won't open, close other audio apps (DJ software, DAWs) and press Apply.
- Plug in a mixer or phono preamp, not a bare turntable: no RIAA EQ is applied.


HOW IT WORKS

1. Album:  pick the record from your collection (or search Discogs).
2. Record: press Record and drop the needle. Hands-free (on by default) starts when the
   music does, pauses at the end of each side (it beeps: flip the record), and stops by
   itself after the last side. "Whole LP" records every side in one take.
3. Split & save: tracks are found automatically. Scroll to zoom, drag lines to adjust,
   Space to play/stop. Choose FLAC and/or MP3, then Save.
   Wrong tracklist, or played side B first? Use "Edit tracklist..." or "played: B -> A".
4. Labels: print sleeve labels (Avery 4x2 / 4x3 sheets or 4" thermal rolls). Every saved
   album is queued, so you can print a whole sheet at once.

Saved albums are also added to rekordbox.xml in your save folder. In Rekordbox:
Preferences > Advanced > Database > rekordbox xml, choose that file once.

Music is saved to Music/Vinyl Rips (change it in File > Settings).
Raw recordings are kept in Music/Vinyl Rips/_recordings - delete them when you're happy.
